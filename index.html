About MedRemind

Welcome to MedRemind, a clean and lightweight web tool designed to help you stay on top of your health. Missing medications can disrupt your recovery; our goal is to eliminate that issue entirely through simple local scheduling. No complicated setups, no data selling, just your personal health schedule preserved right in your browser.

Features

  • 🔐 Local Accounts: Securely sign up and manage your logs directly on your browser.
  • 📋 Real-Time Dashboard: Instantly view your catalog of entered medicines.
  • ⏰ Dynamic Tracking: Seamlessly add medicine names, custom dosages, and times.
  • 🚫 Zero Backend Hassle: Run everything smoothly without needing Node.js, Java, or SQL.
